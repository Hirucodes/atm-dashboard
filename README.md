📊 Power BI Dashboard – ATM Transaction & Revenue Analysis
Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard was developed to analyze ATM operations and their impact on financial performance and operational efficiency across different regions. The project demonstrates how raw operational data can be transformed into actionable business insights.

Focus Areas

🏦 ATM revenue and transaction performance across regions

⚙️ Maintenance and operational cost analysis

📈 Profit margin and uptime comparison

💳 Identification of high and low-performing ATMs

🧭 Regional trends and efficiency improvement opportunities

Analyst Insights

This analysis highlights how data can uncover performance gaps and help decision-makers focus on areas that drive the most value. Key insights include:

Identifying underperforming ATMs impacting overall efficiency

Detecting cost patterns linked to maintenance and operations

Observing transaction trends to understand customer usage behavior

Highlighting opportunities for operational optimization

Business Value

📊 Improves visibility of financial performance

🎯 Identifies operational inefficiencies and optimization opportunities

💡 Supports data-driven decision-making for resource planning

🛠️ Strengthens business intelligence via interactive reporting
